# Model and Asset Attributions & Licenses

## Virtual Laboratories (Manara Academy)

All 3D assets, procedural geometry generators, materials, and textures used in Manara Academy's 3D Virtual Labs adhere strictly to permissive and CC0 / Public Domain licenses.

### 1. Solar System & Planetary Textures
- **Source:** Solar System Scope (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) & NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory / USGS Astrogeology Science Center.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) / NASA Public Domain.
- **Attribution:** Solar System Scope textures distributed under CC BY 4.0. NASA planetary cartography and elevation maps are in the public domain. Procedural fallbacks and canvas shaders are engineered natively for high performance.

### 2. Laboratory Environment & Equipment
- **Procedural 3D Models:** Laboratory bench, wooden and metallic finishes, shelving units, pegboards, glassware (beakers, Erlenmeyer flasks, graduated cylinders, burettes, test tubes), Bunsen burners, electrical components (batteries, multimeters, rheostat, light bulbs, knife switches), and molecular ball-and-stick representations are generated procedurally using Three.js native primitives, CSG/lathe geometries, and custom PBR shaders.
- **License:** MIT / CC0 Public Domain.
- **Author:** Manara Academy Engineering Team.

### 3. Fonts and Icons
- **Lucide Icons:** ISC License (Lucide Contributors).
- **Arabic Typography:** Open source Arabic fonts bundled with Manara Academy platform.
